Our Heritage & Leadership

Deep Local Roots. Global Authority.

Founded in Beirut in 1985 and an active member of the PKF International network since 1992, PKF Chartouni has stood as a bastion of integrity, technical mastery, and trusted client partnerships.

Four Decades of Excellence

Advancing Lebanese Business Through Every Economic Chapter

PKF Chartouni was established with a singular mission: to provide Lebanese enterprises with institutional-grade audit and accounting services governed by the highest ethical and technical standards.

In 1992, the firm expanded its reach by joining PKF International Limited, a world-renowned network of independent accounting firms across 150 countries. This alliance enabled our clients to combine intimate knowledge of Lebanese commercial, fiscal, and banking frameworks with the cross-border capabilities required for regional growth.

Today, as businesses face complex multi-currency environments, hyperinflation accounting standards (IAS 29), and evolving tax laws, our senior partners lead every engagement with unwavering precision.
