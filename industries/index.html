International NGOs & Donor Organizations

Critical Practice

Lebanon hosts one of the largest concentrations of humanitarian and development missions in the region. PKF Chartouni conducts rigorous statutory and project-specific audits in strict compliance with donor grant agreements, including USAID, European Union (DG ECHO, DEVCO), UN agencies (UNHCR, UNDP), and bilateral donors.

• Grant expenditure verification
• Procurement and tender compliance reviews
• Dual currency conversion tracing
• Anti-fraud & internal control assessments

Family Businesses & Private Conglomerates

Generational Governance

Family enterprises form the economic backbone of Lebanon and the diaspora. As businesses transition from founding generations to second- and third-generation leadership, we provide the governance framework, family charters, and holding company structures that safeguard harmony and enterprise longevity.

• Family governance & board charters
• Holding company consolidation
• Inter-generational succession planning
• Shareholder exit dispute resolution

Trading, FMCG & Distribution

Supply Chain & Inventory

Commercial distributors in Lebanon operate in a demanding environment shaped by volatile customs exchange rates, port logistics, and multi-currency pricing. We assist leading importers and retail networks with inventory valuation, margin analysis, and tax audits.

• Cost of goods sold (COGS) reconciliations
• Customs tariff & VAT compliance
• Physical inventory count oversight
• Working capital & credit limit controls

Technology & High-Growth Startups

Cross-Border Scale

Lebanon’s tech entrepreneurs frequently develop products locally while holding intellectual property and raising venture capital through Delaware, UK, or UAE vehicles. We establish clean intercompany transfer pricing, stock option schemes, and investor due diligence packages.

• Venture capital due diligence readiness
• Transfer pricing between Beirut dev team & overseas IP
• UAE Freezone & Delaware subsidiary accounting
• IFRS cap table & option accounting
